## About me

Hi, I'm **João Biffi**. I enjoy building useful software and bringing ideas to life through web development and games. I'm currently working on **Kairós Interactive**, where I'm developing the studio's website and its first game.

My main focus is **ORIGIN · Breath of Light**, an exploration and platforming game currently in Alpha. I like learning by building, refining interfaces and taking projects from an early idea to something people can use and play.

## What I'm working on

**ORIGIN · Breath of Light** is the first game from Kairós Interactive and is currently in Alpha. I'm also building the studio's website and continuing to develop my skills in front-end development, game development and software engineering.

## Featured projects

### [ORIGIN · Breath of Light](https://github.com/JoaoAntonio08/ORIGIN)
A 2D platforming and exploration game about carrying light through a world in the making. Explore connected areas, discover abilities and uncover the story.

`Unity 6.3` `Windows` `Alpha`

### [Kairós Interactive website](https://github.com/JoaoAntonio08/Kairos-site)
The website for my studio, created to showcase its original games and digital projects through an immersive, chapter-based experience.

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Tailwind CSS`

### [Lixeira Tech](https://github.com/JoaoAntonio08/LixeiraTech)
A sustainability platform focused on responsible e-waste disposal, community participation and environmental education.

`React` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL`

### [EconRadar](https://github.com/JoaoAntonio08/EconRadar)
A web project for exploring economic data, market indicators and AI-assisted insights.

`HTML` `JavaScript` `Python` `FastAPI`

### [call-dos-games](https://github.com/JoaoAntonio08/call-dos-games)
A community platform for voice, chat, screen sharing and clips.
